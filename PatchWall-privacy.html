PatchWall Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

PatchWall respects your privacy. This policy describes how your information is handled when you use the app.

Data Collected by the App

PatchWall does not require registration and does not collect personal data such as name, email address, phone number, or precise location. Gameplay progress and settings are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to the developer.

Advertising

PatchWall uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to serve advertisements. AdMob may collect and use the following data to provide relevant ads and measure ad performance:

  • Device identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID and, on iOS, the Identifier for Vendor (IDFV)
  • IP address
  • Device type and operating system version
  • Ad interactions (views, clicks)

Note for iOS users: PatchWall does not request tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency). As a result, Apple's Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) is never accessed. Google Mobile Ads may still collect the device's Identifier for Vendor (IDFV) for ad delivery and analytics. Only contextual (non-personalized) ads are served on iOS.

You can opt out of interest-based advertising at any time:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization
  • iOS: No action required — PatchWall does not collect the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA), so ads are already non-personalized.

For more information about how Google handles data, see Google Privacy Policy and AdMob Data Safety.

User Consent (GDPR / EEA)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK, PatchWall displays a consent dialog before loading ads, powered by Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). You may accept or decline personalized advertising. If you decline, only contextual (non-personalized) ads will be shown.

Children's Privacy

PatchWall is not directed at children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided personal data through the app, we will take steps to delete it. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal data, contact us at the email below.

Changes To This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Support

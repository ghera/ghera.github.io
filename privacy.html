PatchWall Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

PatchWall respects your privacy. This app does not collect, store, or share any personal data. All functionality runs entirely on your device, and no information is transmitted to external servers.

Data Collected

PatchWall does not require user registration and does not collect personal data such as name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

Gameplay Data

PatchWall may store gameplay-related data locally on your device, such as level progress and settings. This information is not transmitted to the developer.

Third-Party Services

PatchWall does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or crash reporting services.

Permissions

PatchWall does not request sensitive permissions that access personal user data.

Changes To This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Support

If you have questions or feedback about PatchWall, you may contact the developer at ghera@libero.it.
